About Me

A tutor who codes, and a coder who teaches.

I'm a 5th-semester BS Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence) student at the University of Arid Agriculture, Gujrat, with a CGPA of 3.64/4.00. Alongside my degree, I've spent the last three years tutoring students one-on-one in computer science and programming — and more recently, applying what I teach through real AI/ML projects during an internship at DecodeLabs.

I believe the best tutors are the ones still actively learning and building. Every concept I teach — from loops to machine learning — is something I've implemented myself, which means I can explain not just the "how" but the "why," and troubleshoot the exact confusion a student is stuck on.

I don't just want my students to pass an exam — I want them to actually understand the logic, so they can build things on their own long after our lessons end.
— Alina Zubair
